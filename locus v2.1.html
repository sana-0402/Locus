The catalog, as a galaxy

Every category is a sun. Every database is a point orbiting it. Suns that sit near each other tend to get reached for on the same kinds of problems. Leave it alone and the whole thing keeps turning.

Drag to pan · scroll or pinch to zoom · hover a point for its name · click to open it. Within each system, the middle "habitable" orbit holds the database Locus judges most central to that category; the rest fan outward from newest (inner) to most dated (outer).

Catalog compiled and condensed from Wikipedia's List of biological databases. Descriptions here are short summaries for quick orientation — visit each database directly for full documentation, citation guidance, and terms of use. Orbit position (era) and sun-to-sun spacing (topical overlap) are Locus's editorial estimates for navigation, not verified data.
